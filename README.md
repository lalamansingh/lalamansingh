<details>
<summary><b>💡 The 0→1 Problem-Solving Playbook</b> (Click to expand)</summary>
<br />

1. **Deconstruct the Core Pain:** Identify exactly who experiences the friction, how severe it is, and whether it represents an urgent priority.
2. **Attack the Root Cause:** Use first-principles thinking and the 5-Whys to disregard surface noise and solve the underlying problem.
3. **Define Measurable Outcomes:** Establish clear North Star metrics and business KPIs before building features.
4. **Prioritize Sharply:** Balance impact, confidence, and engineering effort using RICE scoring and clear P0/P1 scope boundaries.
5. **Ship the Smallest Valuable Version:** Test core assumptions quickly with a targeted MVP to gather real-world signal.
6. **Learn & Iterate from Telemetry:** Use quantitative analytics and user feedback to inform subsequent sprint cycles.

</details>
