## 👋 About me

I'm a product person who likes to build, not just spec. I'm currently an **AI Product Intern at [IndusLabs](https://induslabs.io)**, working on voice AI agents that speak India's languages.

Most of what I know came from shipping fast: 24-hour hackathons, business-plan pitches, and building **Hardini**, a multilingual voice AI platform that gives farmers crop, price, weather and scheme information in their own language.

- 🎯 **Focus:** product thinking, user-centric design, AI products
- 🗣️ **Currently building:** voice AI agents at IndusLabs
- 🌾 **Side project:** Hardini, voice AI for Indian agriculture
- 🚀 **Startup:** Aspirofy, mentorship and career guidance for students
- 📍 **Based in:** Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India (He/Him)
